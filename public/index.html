About VitaBite

Minimalist, transparent nutrition intelligence powered by verified nutritional data and Google Gemini AI.

Our Philosophy

Most nutrition apps are cluttered with aggressive ads, gamification, and fluorescent charts. VitaBite is built for those who value clarity, elegance, and verified data.

We combine deterministic, laboratory-backed food compositions with the natural language and computer vision strengths of Google Gemini AI.

Data Transparency

We never guess without telling you. Common Indian staples are verified against standard food composition tables. Raw and cooked states (like uncooked rice vs. boiled rice) are clearly distinguished.

When an ingredient is uncataloged, Gemini provides an approximate estimation clearly labeled as AI-estimated.

Verified Staples in Database

Rice (Cooked & Raw), Roti / Chapati, Dal / Lentils (Cooked & Raw), Sattu (Roasted Gram Flour), Paneer (Raw & Cooked), Soya Chunks (Boiled & Raw), Milk (Cow & Full Cream), Oats, Potato (Boiled), Banana, Curd / Yogurt, Peanut Butter, Boiled Eggs, Chicken Breast, Chana, Idli, Dosa, Poha, Upma, etc.
